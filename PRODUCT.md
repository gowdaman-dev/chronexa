## Users

Primary decision-makers and day-to-day managers at mid-size and large organisations — HR and admin managers running attendance and access, facility/operations heads running canteens, queues and visitor control, and project leads tracking assignments. They evaluate Chronexa when manual/spreadsheet-driven workforce operations have become error-prone, slow or unverifiable.

## Product Purpose

Chronexa is a workforce-management platform suite. Five integrated products — TimePro (attendance), VisitPro (visitor management), MealPro (canteen/meal), QueuePro (queue management), ProjectPro (projects) — unify time, access, meals, queues and project work onto one verifiable system.

## Positioning

One platform, five workflows, every employee verified by biometrics. Neighboring point tools cover one workflow; Chronexa's mechanism is a single verified workforce layer across time, visitors, meals, queues and projects, with biometric confirmation at every touchpoint and live syncing everywhere.

## Operating Context

HR/admin users work across distributed teams, multiple branches and field employees. Daily workflows: biometric clock-in/out, shift and leave handling, visitor pre-registration and touchless check-in, meal entitlements verified at canteen counters, self-service queue kiosks with QR/SMS ticketing, and project assignment/deadline tracking. They review live dashboards and get real-time announcements.

## Product Principles

- One verified layer, five workflows: every Chronexa story starts from the unified workforce layer, never a single isolated feature.
- Show the system working: demonstrate attendance/queue/meal data in action rather than restating claims.
- Trust through specificity: real product names, real workflows, labeled synthetic data; never invented customers, benchmarks or claims.
- Biometric verification as the differentiator: accuracy and auditability are the proof.
- The brand red #b40000 is the commitment; green stays reserved for status/signal data.
